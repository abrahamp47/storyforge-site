# The Royce Trilogy (1911–1913)

Three interconnected events forming Royce's backstory, told as flashback/prequel content.

## #1: Coffin Rebirth (1911)

[[Randall Kilgrahm]] emerges from hibernation and attacks the [[Khinning]] royal family. King Levant is gravely wounded. [[Levy von Levenhawk]] is crowned at age 18.

## #2: New Dawn (1912)

**Part 1 — "What is royalty to the stateless?"**

1. Reports of assassin vampires reach the court
2. Levy takes this seriously — if vampires, they lead to Randall; if human mimics, their technique helps defend against Randall
3. Network of informants set up to track appearances
4. **Boris Erlang** and a Black Orchid orphan are spotted at a bar
5. Captured by Royce and Levy's squad
6. Boris is interrogated using **Silas's** mind-reading ability (mana-based space)
7. Boris absorbs all the mana in the interrogation space, blasts his own hand off below the wrist, breaks Silas's concentration
8. Boris escapes using **Elven Augment**, saves the orphan
9. Levy lets him go (tactically)
10. Squad tracks half the way to the orphanage using clues
11. Attacked by **Black Orchid Orphans**
12. Levy is taken hostage to **Master [[Quinton Keinz]]'s Orphanage Temple**

King Levant dies this year. Death of Levant confirmed.

## #3: Another World's Lament (1913)

Levy dies. Regency of Lance begins. The circumstances connect to the [[The Black Hand]] and the broader conspiracy. Royce is left behind — Levy's letter asks him to watch over Louis.

## Key Characters Introduced

- **Boris Erlang** — Father of Milo Erlang (UYM student). Uses Elven Augment. Connected to Black Orchid.
- **Silas Eckhardt** — Mind reader. "Kraken Bound." Security specialist. Part of Levy's squad.
- **Quinton Keinz** — SS-tier fighter. SPECTR-connected. Runs orphanage.
