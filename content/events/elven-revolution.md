# The Elven Revolution

The overthrow of [[Elven La]]'s Pureblood ruling system by [[Yozaki Van Krill]] and his half-blood/immigrant supporters.

## Causes

- Ark's Law excluded non-Elven Hope clans from power
- Discriminatory laws against Drows and immigrants
- Ishiguro excommunication demonstrated the system's inflexibility
- "Elven KKK" vigilante attacks on half-blood neighborhoods
- Police killed half-bloods who defended themselves against Klan attacks

## Key Events

1. Yozaki proposes "New Ark's Law" — gains popular following
2. Imprisoned for marching on the capitol; later released
3. Arkin Lee Yang (Pureblood ally) helps him rebuild
4. Klan attacks escalate; half-blood self-defense killings punished by police
5. Half-blood uprising — attacked the capital, took Heroes prisoner
6. Jinshen-to-Lee-Yang transition provides opportunity for coup
7. Jinshens murdered by commoners; Lee Yangs drow'd and executed
8. Yozaki declared Baron (~1913)

## Aftermath

- Round Table rejects Baron Yozaki — Elven La loses its seat
- Remaining Purebloods and Lee Yang survivors flee (some to [[Khinning]])
- Seven Drow Alliance forms in resistance; massacred in Blue Krill Hills
- Lost Lee Yang prince hidden (later possibly [[Tansen Li Wei]])
- **Zanzibar Massacre** (1904) during expansion phase kills 100,000+ — "Firebrand" child born in fire
