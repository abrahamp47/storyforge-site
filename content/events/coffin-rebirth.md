# Coffin Rebirth (1911)

A pivotal event in [[Khinning]]'s history. King Levant (Lavoisier von Levenhawk) is grievously wounded, triggering the premature coronation of his son [[Levy von Levenhawk]] at age 18.

## Context

[[Randall Kilgrahm]], the Vampire King created by Levant's own extermination order, attacks the royal family. The "Coffin" likely refers to Randall's emergence from hibernation (his regenerative sleep after his sister consumed half his body).

## Consequences

- King Levant wounded (dies the following year in "New Dawn")
- Levy crowned at 18 — youngest Levenhawk king
- Sets off the chain of political instability that defines Khinning for the next two decades
- Coincides with Round Table Summit at Gleadenhyme (1911)
