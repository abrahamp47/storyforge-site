# Keinzmen

The manaless humans of [[Troha]]. Among the three species, manaless variants exist: **Keinz** (human) and **Drow** (elf).

## Origins

The **Keinzlandt clan** of [[Harpei]] was prosecuted during the Plague of 290s for allegedly spreading it. Their people were "marked and their mana sealed" — creating a hereditary manaless condition. Over time they became known as the Keinzmen.

## Social Status

- Cannot use mana or any Stream discipline
- Face discrimination ("hateful eyes of Khinningfolk")
- Many migrated away from Harpei to escape prejudice
- Those remaining maintain tightly-knit communities
- To mana-users, Keinz are "a door forever closed, a song they cannot hear"

## Ideological Significance

[[Randall Kilgrahm]]'s vampiric philosophy specifically references the Keinz: "A world of equivalence, where the weakest Keinz and the strongest Manathist are one and the same." Vampirism is framed as the great equalizer between the gifted and the manaless.

## Elven Equivalent

Among elves, the manaless are called **Drow**. In [[Elven La]], Drows are banned from positions of power and can be "made into" drows as punishment (mana stripping).
