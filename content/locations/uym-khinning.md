# UYM Khinning

**Khinning University for Young Manathists** — the premier magical academy in [[Troha]], located in [[Khinning]]. Principal: **Jormen Micolash**.

## Admissions

- Competitive selection exam: 5,000+ candidates
- Stream allotment begins April 2nd; classes commence April 6th
- Students from all Five Nations attend

## Streams Offered

| Stream | Title | Specialty at Khinning |
|--------|-------|----------------------|
| Sorcery | The Philosophers' Stream | Fire elemental; applications in medicine, energy, civil, forensics |
| Conjury | The Naturalists' Stream | Transformation and sealing; crown jewel of Khinning |
| Alchemy | The Healers' Stream | Creative chemistry; medicine advancement |
| Tinkery | The Thinkers' Stream | Mana-powered devices, weapons, armour, vehicles |
| Voyancy | The Hunters' Stream | Sensory/tracking abilities |
