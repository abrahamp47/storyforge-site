# Harpei — The Sunken City

A province of [[Khinning]], located within a massive crater formed by ancient magnetite mining. The "second oddest province in Khinning."

## History

~2000 years ago, the **Magnetite Rush** created an abyss of ~1,700 km² from plateau to sub-sea levels. Owned by two clans:
- **Aurelius** — Largely wiped out during the Plague of 290s
- **Keinzlandt** — Prosecuted for spreading the plague; their people marked, mana sealed. Over time became the **Keinzmen** (manaless folk).

The collapse may have been caused by unearthing a Kraken, whose core was sealed within the mine. A city was built over the ruins to hide the failure.

## Present Day

- Tightly-knit Keinzmen community remains
- Local racketeers control the market: casinos, gambling rings, prize fighting
- Government policies effectively abolished within the city
- Prices hiked for outsiders
- In shadow of surrounding hills by day; illuminated by the Shining Temple of [[Khinning|Godfell]] at night

## Poetry

> "Even the sky is bloody in these areas." — Carriage driver to Alex

John Jacob Bartholomew's poem speaks of Harpei as "lonely," "dim," and home to the "traitor's kin."

## Kraken Theory

Theorists believe a kraken was unearthed by miners; its waking collapsed the mines. The Aurelius and Keinzlandt families stopped mining out of fear of activating the Kraken's Core sealed within.

## In the Novel

Alex, Aron, Lark, and Derek visit Harpei (Chapters 19–22):
- Accessed via skylift from Central Khinning
- Visit **The Golden Ring** — a fancy establishment where Louis, Billy, and Johnny Sabat are also dining
- **Magnetite effect**: The massive mine beneath Harpei creates strong magnetic fields that pull metallic swords downward. Louis exploits this in combat (striking downward for extra force). Alex discovers the trick during their fight.
- The confrontation between Lark and Louis erupts here, with Alex defending Louis using his Gloomshard Blade's broadened form.
- After the fight, Louis agrees to train Alex in swordsmanship.
