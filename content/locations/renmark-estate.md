# Renmark Estate

The residence of [[Royce Garett]] (full name: Royce "Jariel" Garett Renmark) in [[Khinning]], where his students live during their time at [[UYM Khinning]].

## Residents

- **Royce J. Garett** — Owner/mentor
- **[[Alexander Gloomshard]]** — Student
- **[[Emilie Gloomshard]]** — Student
- **[[Aron Linefeldt]]** — Student
- **[[Nora Linefeldt]]** — Student
- **[[Louis von Levenhawk]]** — Lives secretly in the attic
- **Maggie** — Elderly housekeeper/servant

## Features

- Large backyard with short grass (training area)
- Trophy room containing:
  - Painting of 1911 Round Table Summit (Royce, Lance, Levy, Marcus, Levant, Baron Jin Shen, Iscariot Pendragon, Korvan Lee Yang, Connor Marcus, Aegis Elfman, Reynold Westbrook, King of Krillma)
  - Photograph of Alex shaking hands with Levy
  - Photograph of Royce, Levy, and Quinton
  - Medal: "For Plague-time Services in Honour of King Levy von Levenhauk"
  - Large portrait of Robert Renmark and Royce Renmark
  - Service patches, certificates
- Attic (Louis's hidden quarters)
- Mounted taxidermy in hallway

## Significance

The Renmark Estate serves as the domestic setting for the school arc — a found-family home where students from different nations and backgrounds live together under Royce's mentorship. The trophy room is a key plot device revealing backstory connections.
