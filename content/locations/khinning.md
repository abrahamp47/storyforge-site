# Khinning

One of the Five Principal Nations of [[Troha]]. A kingdom ruled by the Levenhawk dynasty. Home of [[UYM Khinning]], the premier university for young manathists.

## Provinces

1. **Central Khinning / Levenhawk** — The capital. Rich areas with spires and towers, crowded marketplaces, cobblestone alleys, the magical academy, the royal palace and adjacent manor.
2. **Maelfrey** — Borders [[Elven La]]. Trade hub with higher elven population. Easy access from Elven La and [[Gleadenhyme]].
3. **Godfell** — Religious province. Conservative, scholarly. Church rights from a sacred site. Home of the Shining Temple.
4. **Carcossa** — Named after a smaller region within. Contains a giant Kraken carcass (bones forming caverns and blocks). H.R. Giger-inspired architecture. Contains the Heart of the Kraken temple.
5. **Harpei** — The "Sunken City." Former magnetite mining center. Pubs, street life, gambling. Controlled by local racketeers. Keinzmen community. Inspiration: Goa meets mob stories.
6. **Blemer** — Setting for Valence Spencer's solo investigation arc.
7. **Dinsprey** — Forest district neighboring Harpei and Blemer. Home of the Du Bois family.

Additional named provinces: Elsatine, Haccarus, Senteagal, Macassida, Farealus.
