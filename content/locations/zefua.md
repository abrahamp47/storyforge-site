# Zefua — Sodvic Republic

The 12th non-principal country, allied with [[Gleadenhyme]]. A Sodvic settlement in the woods between Wyndale and [[Khinning]] Central.

## Description

- **Exterior**: Fortified wooden pickets with charred bronze flags bearing black Sodvic symbols. Ominous appearance.
- **Interior**: Vibrant and colorful. Blocks of buildings lit by yellow lanterns, decorated with vertical flags in vivid colors depicting Sodvic deities and priests. People dressed in extremely vivid colors.
- **Culture**: Sodvic (followers of Dansodo). Emphasizes peaceful coexistence. Has a holy noble chief, hunters, and cooks.

## Key Features

- Bathhouse (large communal bath)
- Guest quarters (small houses with low beds)
- Hunting grounds in surrounding woods (Moon Whites, Tatzelwurms)
- Gravel paths

## Fauna

- **Moon Whites** — White-furred deer with white antlers and ruby red eyes. Dangerous (antler charges). Hunted for venison.
- **Tatzelwurms** — Cat-headed serpents. Poisonous (Sodsroth venom causes paralysis). Found in surrounding Zefuan woods.

## Plot Significance

Alex's first night away from home. He hunts his first Moon White with Royce using a rope dart — a formative experience that helps him decide on Conjury. Emilie overcomes her fear of Sodvics. The settlement demonstrates that feared "devil worshippers" are actually peaceful, colorful people.

## Key Personnel

- **The Chief** — Unremarkable man in late 40s, greying hair, hoarse voice. Knowledgeable in medicine/poison identification.
- **[[Kenichi Yagurai]]** — Grand Guard, friend of Royce.
