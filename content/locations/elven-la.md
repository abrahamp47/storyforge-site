# Elven La

One of the Five Principal Nations of [[Troha]]. Translates to "Land of Elves" in traditional elvish. Originally given to The Society of Elven Hope as a safe haven for elves after Troha's division.

## Governing System: Ark's Law

Devised by **Ark Jin Shen**, leader of the Society of Elven Hope at formation:
- 30 original clans constituted the Elven Hope
- Kingship rotates every 10 generations between clans
- During transition, the next family's heir is selected by **Heroship** (philanthropy, wartime bravery, tactical brilliance)
- Kings are called **Barons**; Knights are called **Heroes**
- Only Elven Hope clans had rights to the throne — immigrants and half-bloods excluded

## The Revolution

The system worked for 150 generations until demographic shifts created conflict:
1. **Ishiguro Clan** — shifted from Elven to Lith; excommunicated for instigating uprising about exclusion
2. Discriminatory laws followed: Drows banned from power, immigrants couldn't own land
3. **[[Yozaki Van Krill]]** proposed the "New Ark's Law" — argued the founder intended to add new families
4. Imprisoned, released, then launched coup during Jinshen-to-Lee-Yang transition
5. Murdered/drow'd opponents; took title of Baron (~1913)

## Key Events

- **Zanzibar Massacre** (1904): Yozaki's army kills 100,000+ at Drogba Festival. The "Firebrand" child survives.
- **Seven Drow Alliance**: Remnants of resistance clans hiding in the Blue Krill Hills after the revolution.
- **Walled City Lockdown** (1912): Yozaki consolidates control.

## Species & Social Structure

- **Purebloods**: Original 30 Elven Hope families (higher elves, purple-skinned)
- **Half-bloods**: Mixed heritage or immigrant elves
- **Drows**: Manaless elves — banned from positions of power
- **Liths**: Stone/earth-based species; some clans shifted from Elf to Lith over generations

## Round Table Status

Rejected Baron Yozaki's claim — Elven La has **no seat** at the Round Table during his reign (1913–1927).
