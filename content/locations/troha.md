# Troha

The world in which the story takes place. A realm where mana is as natural as breath.

## Species

| Species | Manaless Variant | Notes |
|---------|-----------------|-------|
| **Humans** | Keinz | Most populous |
| **Elves** | Drow | Pointed ears; sub-types include rock elves, purple-skinned high elves |
| **Liths** | — | Stone/earth-based beings; originate from Ravenbrook |

## The Five Principal Nations

1. **[[Khinning]]** — Kingdom; Levenhawk dynasty; center of magical education
2. **[[Gleadenhyme]]** — Kingdom; Gloomshard dynasty; protagonist's homeland
3. **[[Elven La]]** — Elven nation; Baron/Baroness title; revolutionary upheaval
4. **[[Krillma]]** — Kingdom; natural beauty and mineral wealth
5. **[[Volta]]** — Least detailed; borders Elven La and Krillma

## Non-Principal Nations

Numerous smaller states. Known examples:
- **Zanzibar** — Independent non-principal state southwest of Elven La; conquered by Greater Elven La (1904)

## Governance

The [[Round Table]] governs international relations, with a rotating Summit and the [[SPECTR]] unit for enforcement.

## Fundamental Concepts

- **Mana** — Universal spiritual energy; basis of all magical disciplines ([[Streams]])
- **Manathist** — A mana practitioner
- All beings born to mana's flow except the manaless (Keinz/Drow)
