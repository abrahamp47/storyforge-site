# Gleadenhyme

One of the Five Principal Nations of [[Troha]]. A kingdom ruled by the Gloomshard dynasty. Home of protagonist [[Alexander Gloomshard]].

## Provinces

1. **Redford** — The capital. Rich areas with spires and towers, crowded marketplaces, cobblestone alleys. Has a gilded statue of Great Edmund Clive Gloomshard in the town square. Train station serves The Mayflower.
2. **Wyndale** — Borders [[Elven La]]. Trade hub with higher elven population.
3. **Wes Curosov** — Religious/conservative province with scholars. Sacred site grants additional church rights.

## Culture

- Ceremonial knighting tradition for departing princes
- The Gloomshard family crest features a lion
- Strong tradition of antelope herding on the moors
- Royal Alchemist position (merit-based)

## Military

- **Zephyr Grand Guard: Tempest Corps** — Naval unit. Aliases: Sea Dogs, Ardent Guard, The Crazies. Equipped with ships, freighters, harpoons, mana packs. Recruits from Sorcery (Wind, Crystal, Water), Manatechnology, and Conjury (Aquatic Forms, Sealing).

## Key Locations

- **Gloomshard Manor** — Royal residence with moor, moat, drawbridge
- **Redford Station** — Departure point for The Mayflower train
