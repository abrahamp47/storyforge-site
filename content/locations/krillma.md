# Krillma

One of the Five Principal Nations of [[Troha]]. Known for its natural beauty, mineral wealth, and a gluttonous king.

## Key Locations

- **Bjorngard Palace** — Main royal palace
- **Ravenbrook** — A ravine valley on the northern border with [[Volta]]. Fed by glaciers from Northridge. Former haven for wildlife including Tursimians and Lithkind. Devastated by the King's hunting (1907–1912). Documented by explorer Rembrandt J. Vanheim.
- **Northridge** — Glacial mountains forming the northern boundary
- **Dinsprey** — Forest region (borders Khinning's Dinsprey province)

## Fauna (Historical)

- **Tursimians** — Believed to originate from Ravenbrook; primordial species
- **Moon Whites** — Hunted to extinction from Krillman soil
- **Rainbow Dace** — Hunted to extinction from Krillman soil

## Notable Families

- Bernhardt, Linefeldt, Eckhardt, Ayekomov, Berzgrandt (from Families list)
