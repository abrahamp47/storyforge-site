# Volta

One of the Five Principal Nations of [[Troha]]. The least detailed of the five nations in the current source material.

## Known Facts

- Hosts the Round Table Summit on rotation (first in 1883, then every 20 years: 1903, 1923)
- Borders [[Elven La]], [[Krillma]], and [[Khinning]]
- Notable families: Westbrook, Kinyoun, Elfman, Zorick (Kh-Vol) — from Families list

## Round Table Summit Rotation

Volta → Elven La → Gleadenhyme → Khinning → Krillma (4-year cycle)
