# The Ivory Tower (Arc)

The first major story arc / Book 1 of the Manathist series. Subtitled "A Record of Life and Times of A Certain Prince."

## Setting

Begins in [[Gleadenhyme]] and transitions to [[UYM Khinning]] in 1923. [[Alexander Gloomshard]] (age 16) enters the university to study as a manathist.

## Structure

- **Chapter 0**: "A New World" — World introduction; mana, species, Keinz/Drow
- **Chapter 1**: "The Letter" — Alex receives UYM admission; family moments; Adrian's tarot (The Chariot)
- **Chapter 2**: "Goodbye Home" — Knighting ceremony; meeting Sir Lawrence; farewell; departure with Royce and Emilie
- **Chapter 3**: "The Mayflower" — Train journey through Redford; crossing the moor; entering Khinning territory through Blue Krill Hills/Seven Drow Alliance territory

## Themes

- Coming of age in a politically unstable world
- The tension between tradition and reform (Alex questions Knighthood tradition with his father)
- Identity and belonging (introvert prince in a social academic environment)
- The political machinations surrounding a young royal abroad
