# Book 1 — The Ivory Tower: Arc Structure

The novel is divided into **14 arcs** across 4 acts + epilogue. Arcs 1–7 have written content; arcs 8–14 are placeholder/planned.

## Act 1: Chapters 1–11 (WC: 30,399)

| Arc | Title | Content |
|-----|-------|---------|
| #1 | **The Journey** | Alex receives letter, knighting, departure, train to Khinning |
| #2 | **Stream Selection** | Arrival at Renmark Estate, discovering trophy room painting, enrollment |
| #3 | **Mana Training** | Mana Activation training, Aron's drow struggle, first private classes |

## Act 2A

| Arc | Title | Content |

## Rough Chapter Beats (cutting room floor)

## Friendship Timeline (Planned)

Lark **actively tries to be Alex's friend** (it's his mission from Lawrence):
1. Selection — Lark and Alex talk
2. Classes — Lark and Aron banter next to Salzabar and Alex
3. Boys night out (carnival ground)
4. Pub outing (Alex can't drink — Gloomshard monk-like laws for royalty)
5. Louis introduction
6. Hunting — both impress but teachers prefer Alex over Lark → **breaking point**
