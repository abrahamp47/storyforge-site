# Tales From Troha

A spinoff anthology series of short stories set in the world of [[Troha]], exploring backstories and side characters.

## Planned/Written Stories

### 1. All's Good in Harpei (AGIH)
**Setting**: [[Harpei]], 1916 (post-Blackheart riots and vampire epidemic)
**Genre**: Noir/crime
**Protagonist**: Unnamed crystal sorcerer MC (former Gleadish military, quit smoking, carries empty lighter, wraps forearms in numbing tincture nightly due to pain from crystal sorcery)
**Plot**: The Ring (crime syndicate led by [[Lawrence]]) is losing grip over Harpei as other factions rise. MC investigates raised repayment rates (150% → higher) and uncovers a conspiracy involving:
- **The Ring** (Lawrence L. Burnhardt's organization)
- **The Keinz Blackhearts** (led by Quinton Keinz Joey)
- **The Noblemen's Circle** (plague conspirators)
- A cult trying to access mines The Ring protects

**Chapter 1 — The Singer's Sin**: MC is called to Arcana Nocturne (nightclub) by bossman (Lawrence). Minister **Kiker** found dead — killed by a teal hairpin through the skull behind the ear. A singer named **Layla** (blonde, blue eyes, purple satin gown) was his companion. MC investigates: no sorcery, wine untampered, no mana residue. Vance (club manager) provides testimony. MC uses voyancy techniques and mana flow assessment.

**Key Events**:
- Conspirators try to assassinate Lawrence; kill Francis; scar Lark
- MC instinctively raises crystal shield to save Lawrence and Lark
- Cult launches all-out fight; MC battles Eli Epcot (Kimblee)
- Lawrence "snap kills" the PMC leader in the finale

### 2. The Dilemma (1652)
**Setting**: Wyndale, [[Gleadenhyme]], 1652 (start of the Plague Epidemic)
**Genre**: Moral drama / short story (~1150 words, complete)
**Protagonist**: A rookie City Guard of Gleadenhyme
**Plot**: Guard investigates cattle killings at Harker's farm. Discovers a man feeding stolen lambs to his vampire daughter (~9-10 years old). The father begs for her life — he infected her deliberately to save her from the plague (plague kills; vampirism only kills in sunlight). The guard faces the dilemma: kill a child vampire, or let the family flee to the woods.
**Themes**: Moral ambiguity of vampirism — echoes [[Randall Kilgrahm]]'s philosophy. The "Hunter's Dilemma" planning doc adds: the family is later caught when the daughter gives in to bloodlust and kills her parents. The guard is left wondering if killing the child would have saved the family.
