# The World of Troha — Source Compendium

Bulk ingest of the complete worldbuilding corpus for **The World of Troha** (working title: *Manathist*), authored by C. K. Morris. This corpus spans 90+ documents including the full novel *The Ivory Tower*, character bibles, lore sheets, timelines, province guides, and draft chapters.

## Narrative Beats

### Book 1: The Ivory Tower
- **Alexander (Alex) Marcus Gloomshard**, Prince of [[Gleadenhyme]], is admitted to [[UYM Khinning]] (University for Young Manathists) ranked 53rd of 5000 candidates.
- Alex departs home with cousin **Emilie Connor Gloomshard** and mentor/driver **Royce J. Garett** (professor of History, red-eyed white-haired man).
- Knighting ceremony before departure; receives the Gloomshard Blade (black steel sword staff with lion crest).
- Journey via carriage through Redford (capital of Gleadenhyme), then train "The Mayflower" to Khinning.
- Alex must choose a **Stream** (magical discipline) — gravitates toward Fire Sorcery.
- Introduction to the political intrigue of the Five Nations through the Round Table Summit system.
- **Lark Burnhardt** is a major antagonist/rival figure — Wendigo conjurer.
- **Lawrence** is a Khinning minister who mentors Lark; chess-based philosophical dialogues reveal their complex mentor/student dynamic.
- Timeline events span from Alex's enrollment (1923) through political upheaval.

### Book 2: Cult of ECAS
- Focuses on a religious antagonist "ECAS" (Ekkes) who founds a heretical church.
- Chapter structure: Sodvic temple massacre → humanity's outlook → The 4 Great Sinners → Kill the King → The Yellow Dahn → new church → investigation → trial.

## World Facts Introduced

- **Troha** is a world where mana is natural; divided into 5 Principal Nations + smaller Non-Principal states governed by the Round Table.
- **Species**: Humans, Elves, Liths (stone/earth-based); manaless variants are Keinz (human) and Drow (elf).
- **Streams** (magic disciplines): Sorcery, Conjury, Alchemy, Tinkery (Manatechnology), Voyancy, Exorcism, Morphing.
- **UYM Khinning** specializes in Fire Sorcery and Conjury.
- **Conjury** involves transformation (taking animal forms) and sealing.
- **Round Table Summit** rotates between nations every 4 years (Volta → Elven La → Gleadenhyme → Khinning → Krillma cycle).
- **SPECTR Unit** — Round Table's clandestine recovery/intelligence unit.
- **Vampirism** exists; vampires experience heightened emotions and suffer bloodlust from starvation.
- **Pantheon**: Denanagen (Blessings) vs Dansodo (Curses); followers are Neiganites vs Sodvics.

## Canon Conflicts (Resolved)

- **Ark's Law succession**: Canonized as **10 generations** before handover.
- **Yozaki's birth name**: Canonized as **Yozaki Li Zhao**. "Li Jhao" is a deprecated spelling.
- **Ark's Law founder**: Canonized as **Ark Jin Shen** (not Kohei Amatsu).
- **Alex's admission rank**: Complete Edition says **53rd**; earlier "Feedback Cuts" draft says **7th**. Use 53rd (final version).
- **Admission letter signer**: Complete Edition says **Jormen Micolash (Principal)**; earlier draft says **Reginald Ludovicus (Director of Grand Magic Council)**. Use Micolash (final version). Ludovicus remains a character (Edward Ludovicus is a student).
- The "June of Changes" document appears to be a real-world political excerpt (Salvador Allende) used as thematic inspiration, not in-world canon.
