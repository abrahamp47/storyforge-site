# Conjury — The Naturalists' Stream

The crown jewel of [[Khinning]]'s magical education. A stream dealing with **transformation** and **sealing**, brought to prominence during King Levi's reign.

## Core Mechanics

### Mana Sleeves (Form Sleeves)
- Magical cards that hold mana in pocket dimensions
- Conjurers hunt beasts, defeat them, convert their forms to mana, and seal them in cards
- Cards are used to evoke transformations
- Cannot sustain living material (transmuting living objects to mana kills them)
- Also used by other streams for storage, minimizing machinery/space (transportation revolution)
- Khinning holds monopoly patent on the technique

### Transformation Process
1. Lift the mana clasp on the Form Sleeve
2. Bright flash of light engulfs the user
3. User enters a dark internal world (pitch black, shallow water on floor)
4. Must connect consciousness to the beast form
5. Physical body transforms externally while consciousness integrates

### Fighting Styles (Academic Classification)
1. **Summoners** — Combinations of summoning weapons + biological knowledge
2. **Polymorphers** — Using beast forms in combat
   - **Shifters**: Low-energy smaller transformations, mid-battle form changes
   - **Nuking**: Single powerful high-energy transformation
