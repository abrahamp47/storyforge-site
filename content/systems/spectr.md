# SPECTR Unit

A clandestine military/intelligence unit that works for the [[Round Table]].

## Mission

Recovery of data, people, things, and corpses of soldiers and persons of interest. Tracks runaway convicts, MIA personnel, and other People of Interest. Described as "glorified thieves."

## Capabilities

- Not the best fighters, but highly effective at retrieval
- Virtually no limitation of jurisdiction — can search anywhere undetected
- Clandestine operations capability

## Authorization

SPECTR can be assigned tasks by:

1. **The Chairman of the Round Table** — must publicly announce the task
2. **A ruler of a Principal country** — requires a vote: must gain at least 3 of 5 votes from Principal rulers + Chairman. The task can be kept secret.

The chance of a ruler's proposal passing is slim, as no country will allow another to run clandestine operations. This makes authorized secret missions extremely rare and politically significant.
