# The Round Table

The international governing body of [[Troha]] — a fantasy equivalent of the United Nations. Comprises the 5 Principal Countries and numerous Non-Principal countries.

## Structure

- **Chairman** of the Round Table
- 5 Principal Country rulers as permanent members
- Non-Principal countries as secondary members

## The Summit

The Round Table Summit rotates between Principal nations on a **4-year cycle**:

**Volta → Elven La → Gleadenhyme → Khinning → Krillma**

Timeline of Summits:
- 1883: Volta
- 1887: Elven La
- 1891: Gleadenhyme
- 1895: Khinning
- 1899: Krillma
- 1903: Volta
- 1907: Elven La
- 1911: Gleadenhyme (pictured)
- 1915: Khinning (Baron out — Elven La excluded)
- 1919: Krillma
- 1923: Volta
- 1927: Krillma

See [[SPECTR]].

## Elven La's Exclusion

After [[Yozaki Van Krill]]'s revolution (~1913), the "regalist Roundtable" rejected Baron Yozaki. Elven La had **no seat** until Baroness Nora Lee Yang's ascension (1927).
