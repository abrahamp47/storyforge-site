# The Pantheon of Troha

The divine hierarchy of [[Troha]], divided into two opposing alignments.

## The Two Alignments

### Denanagen's Pantheon of Blessings
- **Denanagen** ("the one who lived") — First Demigod who lived a human life to understand humanity and judge properly.
- Followers: **Neiganites**
- Reincarnation as **Geni** — opposes Dan's tyranny
- Later reincarnates as **Yanbo** — kind leader of a tribe

### Dansodo's Pantheon of Curses
- **Dansodo** (original demigod form: **Dan**) — Terrorizes harsh people like a dictator
- Followers: **Sodvics**
- Reincarnates as **Tippin** — becomes protector of a tribe
- Children and grandchildren fill out rest of pantheon

## Neutral Figures

- **Heres** — God of Knowledge. Born Sodvic but chooses neutrality. No direct story role.

## The Tragic Cycle

1. **Drocell Lee Han** — Time traveller who tries to marry Denanagen's mother by resetting timeline. Sacrifices her to elder gods out of spite when rejected. Realizes mistake. Becomes powerful enough to threaten Gods.
2. Geni and Dan leave the pantheon; Drocell takes over.
3. When Drocell rules, the universe cycle ends (Ragnarok-like event).
4. A **Demigod in self-imposed exile** returns to ensure humanity's survival.

## The Exiled Demigod

Rejects Godhood because his human mother (a nun) was harassed and murdered by humans for raising a demigod child born "out of nowhere." Concluded that Gods lose their humanity after the Demigod phase.

## In-Story Relevance

- **Sodvic Temple Massacre** — inciting event of Book 2 (Cult of ECAS)
- **Godfell** province of [[Khinning]] is the religious center
- **ECAS/Ekkes** founds a heretical new church (Book 2 antagonist)
