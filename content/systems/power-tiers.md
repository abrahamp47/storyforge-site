# Power Tiers

An author-defined ranking of character combat ability across the series.

## Tier List

| Tier | Characters |
|------|-----------|
| **SS** | Edmund Clive Gloomshard, Quinton Keinz |
| **S** | [[Levy von Levenhawk]], Keon (Berzgrandt), Jinrui (Igarashi) |
| **A** | [[Royce Garett]] |
| **B** | Hades (Elfman), Venessa |
| **C** | [[Lark Burnhardt]], [[Louis von Levenhawk]] |
| **D** | [[Alexander Gloomshard]] |
| **E** | [[Aron Linefeldt]] |

## Notes

- Alex (D-tier) is weaker than his rival Lark (C-tier) — consistent with the narrative where Alex must grow
- Royce (A-tier) is significantly more powerful than his students
- Levy (S-tier) matches his role as a formidable king
- Quinton Keinz at SS despite being a Keinz (manaless) — suggests exceptional non-mana combat
- Edmund Clive Gloomshard (SS) is the legendary "Hunter Warrior King" who personally led wendigo hunts
