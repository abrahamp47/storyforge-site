# Streams

**Streams** are the magical disciplines of [[Troha]]. All manathists are divided into groups based on how they use mana (spiritual energy). Stream selection is a critical life decision made upon entering a magical academy.

## The Six Streams

### Sorcery — The Philosophers' Stream
- Elemental manifestation of mana
- The oldest and most popular stream
- Wide range of career options and advanced specializations
- [[UYM Khinning]] specializes in **Fire Sorcery**
- Quote: "A true master sorcerer is the master of true elemental manifestation of mana." — Darius Bergneal

### Conjury — The Naturalists' Stream
- Relatively new stream, brought to prominence during King Levi's reign
- Two major techniques: **Transformation** and **Sealing**
- Crown jewel of Khinning
- Each conjurer has a specific animal/creature form (e.g., Wendigo, Gangine, Siren, Ent, Ifrit)
- Outdoor-oriented training style
- Quote: "Conjury gives you the power to swim as a siren and to fly as a griffin." — Aeser Ayekmova

### Alchemy — The Healers' Stream
- Age-old stream responsible for medical advances
- Creative chemistry and out-of-the-box thinking
- Notable historical alchemists: Marie Curie, Friedrich Sertürner, Alexander Fleming
- Quote: "The secrets of the universe are hidden in alchemy." — Maria Goeppert-Mayer

### Tinkery (Manatechnology) — The Thinkers' Stream
- Creation of mana-powered devices: weapons, armour, transparent carts, etc.
- Controversial classification — practitioners barely use mana directly themselves
- Also called Manatechnologists
- Quote: "The best way to predict the future is to invent it." — Unknown

### Voyancy — The Hunters' Stream
- Sensory and tracking abilities
- Also called "Foresight Specialists" or "Sensors"
- Considered "boring" by some students

### Exorcism
- Details sparse in source material
- Distinct from Sorcery

### Morphing
- Transformation-based (distinct from Conjury's animal forms)
- Details sparse in source material

## Elven Variant: Hemataugment

**Elven Hemataugment** (Elven Augmentry) — a discipline specific to elves. [[Yozaki Van Krill]] majored in Strength specialization.

## Stream Levels

Characters have stream levels indicating proficiency. Schools set familiarities with multiple streams even if a student specializes in one.
