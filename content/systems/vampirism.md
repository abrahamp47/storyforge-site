# Vampirism

A supernatural condition in [[Troha]] that transforms the afflicted into immortal beings with heightened abilities and dangerous compulsions.

## Properties

- Vampires experience **emotions and senses at a higher rate**
- **Bloodlust** results from starvation — difficult to control
- Can be transmitted through biting
- Vampires can regenerate from extreme damage through hibernation/sleep
- Perpetual life

## Ideological Dimension

[[Randall Kilgrahm]] theorizes that vampirism could create equality:
- Removes the power gap between Manathists and Keinz (manaless)
- Perpetual life means no generational power accumulation
- "A world of equivalence, where the weakest Keinz and the strongest Manathist are one and the same"

## Historical Incidents

- A group of vampires terrorized part of [[Khinning]]
- King Lavoisier ordered their massacre by [[Randall Kilgrahm]]
- A surviving vampire infected Kilgrahm's family as revenge
- The subsequent royal extermination order created the Vampire King
