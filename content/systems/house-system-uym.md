# UYM House System

[[UYM Khinning]] divides students into four houses, each representing a virtue and its shadow.

## The Four Houses

| House | Color | Flower | Virtue | Duality |
|-------|-------|--------|--------|---------|
| **Red Lotus** | Red | Lotus | VICTORY | Tenacity vs Greed |
| **Blue Hyacinth** | Blue | Hyacinth | BRAVERY | Selflessness vs Hubris |
| **Green Zinnia** | Green | Zinnia | PEACE | Logic vs Indifference |
| **Yellow Daffodil** | Yellow | Daffodil | WISDOM | Curiosity vs Sacrificial |
