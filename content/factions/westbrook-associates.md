# Westbrook & Sons Co.

A corporation based in [[Volta]] that has effectively parasitized [[Khinning]]'s economy through a reconstruction scheme.

## The Westbrook Parasite Plan

### Setup (post-1911 Vampire Plague)
1. King Levant is incapacitated; **Wilfred Perrier** (Minister of Foreign Affairs) becomes Regent
2. Perrier is close with **Reynold Westbrook**, head of Westbrook & Sons Co.
3. They collude with Round Table Chairman **Lord Iscariot Pendragon**
4. Round Table sanctions a "reconstruction project" for vampire plague damage

### Mechanism
- Round Table pays Westbrook & Sons Co. in full for reconstruction
- Khinning repays the Round Table in small installments over time
- Westbrook gains monopoly on ALL reconstruction projects in Khinning
- Westbrook imports tools and workforce from Volta (where their main market base is)
- No money circulates within Khinning — all flows to Volta
- Westbrook undercuts other Khinning businesses by working at lower rates
- Eventually controls ~85% of all construction in Khinning

### Secondary Effect: Keinz Displacement
- The [[Keinzmen]] were the main manual workforce in construction and shopkeeping
- When Khinning workers lost construction jobs, magic-users moved into shopkeeping
- Magic-users outcompete Keinz (work longer, faster with mana)
- Drives economic disparity between mana-users and manaless

## Connection to Levy's Sacrifice

From Levy's final letter: "With my death and subsequent crowning of Louis, the Westbrook pact will have been broken." Levy's assassination (1929) and Louis's coronation are deliberately orchestrated to break the Westbrook stranglehold — implying the pact requires a specific Levenhawk king on the throne.

## Key Figures

- **Reynold Westbrook** — Head of the company; attended 1911 Round Table Summit
- **Daniel Westbrook** — Son; sent back in time 4 generations to discover how the family's involvement in Khinning was exposed
- **Wilfred Perrier** — Khinning Regent who enabled the scheme
- **Lord Iscariot Pendragon** — Round Table Chairman, co-conspirator
