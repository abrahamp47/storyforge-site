# House Levenhawk

The ruling dynasty of [[Khinning]], based in the capital province of Levenhawk.

## Succession

| Ruler | Reign | Notes |
|-------|-------|-------|
| King Levant (Lavoisier) | 1884–1911 | Wounded in "Coffin Rebirth"; ordered Kilgrahm massacre |
| King Levy | 1911–1913 | First reign; dies in "Another World's Lament" |
| Regent Lance | 1913–1926 | Brother; dies in "Finding Quinton" |
| Regent Louis | 1926–1927 | Brother; steps down |
| King Levy | 1927–1929 | Second coronation; assassinated in "Empty Crown" |

## The Kilgrahm Tragedy

King Lavoisier ordered [[Randall Kilgrahm]] to massacre a vampire group. When Kilgrahm's family was subsequently infected, Lavoisier ordered their extermination too — creating the vengeful Vampire King who would destroy the royal couple and destabilize the dynasty for decades.

## Political Instability

The rapid succession of rulers (5 changes in 18 years) creates the unstable backdrop for the Ivory Tower-era events at [[UYM Khinning]]. Students attend school under Regent Lance while the kingdom processes trauma from Levy's first death.
