# Seven Drow Alliance

A resistance coalition formed in the aftermath of [[Yozaki Van Krill]]'s takeover of [[Elven La]].

## Formation

Five years after Van Krill annexed Elven La, the **7 Heroes of the Lee Yang family** (potential heirs to the throne) assembled faction groups to overthrow the Baron. The factions were divided and couldn't match the Elven La army.

## The Blue Krill Hills

The factions retreated to the **Blue Krill Hills** (also called Seven Drow Hills — though there are actually only 6 hills). Each hill is named after one of the first 6 faction leaders. The valley is named **Korbak Valley** after the 7th faction leader.

The army surrounded the hills and massacred the resistance. Only those who adapted to guerrilla warfare survived. Of the original seven factions, **only three remain**.

## Current Status

The survivors maintain a guerrilla presence in the Blue Krill Hills, which are now dangerous territory ("even the sky is bloody in these areas"). The carriage route to [[Khinning]] passes through this region.
