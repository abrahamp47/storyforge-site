# House Gloomshard

The ruling dynasty of [[Gleadenhyme]]. Their family crest features a **lion** and their ceremonial weapon is the **Gloomshard Blade** (black steel sword staff with sword breaker, green handle with black accents).

## Notable

- **Edmund Clive Gloomshard** — The "Hunter Warrior King" (SS-tier). Personally led wendigo hunts in Khinno-Gleadish free zones (1811). Has a gilded monument in Redford town square.
- **Alexander becomes King in 1929** at age 22 — coinciding with Levy's assassination in Khinning. This places the novel's epilogue at Alex's coronation.

## Current Family (1923)

- **Marcus Finneas Gloomshard** — King. Large man, diplomatic, emotional beneath composure.
- **Queen** (unnamed) — Interest in paranormal/macabre; her dreams of manathism unfulfilled due to era's gender restrictions.
- **[[Alexander Gloomshard]]** — Crown Prince, heir apparent. Age 16.
- **Adrian Marcus Gloomshard** ("Addy") — Second son. Enthusiastic about tarot/divination.
- **Aveline Gloomshard** ("Avy") — Infant daughter.
- **[[Emilie Gloomshard]]** — Niece/cousin. Father is Royal Alchemist.
- **Claire** — Cousin, provides advice about Khinning.

## Historical Note

There was "once a King, a failure — the only failed King to bear the Gloomshard name." This unnamed ancestor's story connects to themes of perseverance and thinking differently.

## Traditions

- Princes knighted before departing the kingdom
- Family parting ceremony with ceremonial blade bestowal
- Court sessions with ministers
- Rooftop "brooding" (per Alex and Adrian)
