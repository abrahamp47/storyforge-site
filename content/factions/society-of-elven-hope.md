# The Society of Elven Hope

The founding ruling body of [[Elven La]], consisting of 30 original elven clans. Their descendants became known as **Purebloods** or **Higher Elves** (purple-skinned).

## Ark's Law

Devised by **Ark Jin Shen**:
- Kingship rotates between the 30 clans every 10 generations
- Transition heirs selected by **Heroship** (philanthropy, bravery, tactical brilliance)
- Kings titled **Baron**; Knights titled **Hero**
- Only Elven Hope clans eligible for throne

## Decline

Over time, the Society became an entrenched aristocracy:
- Banned Drows (manaless elves) from power
- Restricted immigrant land ownership
- The **Ishiguro Clan** was excommunicated after shifting from Elf to Lith and demanding inclusion
- Klan-like vigilante groups ("Elven KKK") attacked half-blood neighborhoods

## Fall

[[Yozaki Van Krill]]'s revolution (culminating ~1913) destroyed Pureblood rule:
- Jinshens murdered in their sleep by commoners
- Lee Yangs turned into drows and publicly executed (some escaped to anonymity)
- Remaining Purebloods lost political power
