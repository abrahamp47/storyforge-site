# The Black Hand

A covert military faction within [[Khinning]]'s armed forces responsible for the assassination of the royal family.

## Actions

- Attacked the royal palace, killing Louis's parents (King Levy and Queen)
- When Levy went searching for the Black Hand, "his body returned lifeless" (1913 — "Another World's Lament")
- Also connected to Daniel Westbrook's backstory as "Council's Blackhand" — they ambush Dorian Westbrook over hidden family treasure

## Leadership

[[Louis von Levenhawk]] believes **Regent Lance** founded the Black Hand. This is his central suspicion and the reason he hides in Royce's attic rather than live at the palace under Lance's regency.

Royce's response: "It's not as simple as that" — suggesting the truth is more complex than Louis's theory.

## Political Implications

If Lance truly founded the Black Hand:
- He engineered his own ascent to Regent by eliminating Levy
- The "Keinzlandt massacre" is cited as the public explanation for the palace attack
- The truth would make Lance's 13-year regency (1913–1926) illegitimate
