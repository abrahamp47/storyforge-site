# Johnny Sabat

A first-year student at [[UYM Khinning]]. A cunning, persistent provocateur.

## Profile

- **House**: Red Lotus (implied — his fight with Shiro occurs in the House of Carnations)
- **Conjury Form**: Ifrit
- **Appearance**: Lean, slender figure, tattooed neck, angled eyes, dark hair, pointed nose. "Soft foxy voice."
- **Personality**: Cunning, persistent, smiling through pain. Never backs down. A "snake" per Shiro's narration. Dances his "war dance" to provoke opponents.

## Actions

- Nosily tries to access restricted areas (Chapter 20 — the Golden Ring incident)
- Beats up [[Johan Jordis]] to provoke Shiro into a fight
- His brutal fight with Shiro in Chapter 30.5 ends with both bloodied — neither yields
- Uses strategy: got Shiro in a chokehold after absorbing punches, only lost when Shiro headbutted the floor to break free

## Significance

Johnny represents the "fight for respect" theme. His provocation of Shiro mirrors how weaker characters must find unconventional paths to prove themselves. Despite being physically outmatched, he refuses to break — forcing a draw through sheer will.
