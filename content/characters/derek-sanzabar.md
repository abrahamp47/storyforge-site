# Derek Francis Sanzabar

A student at [[UYM Khinning]], childhood friend and "trusted associate" of [[Lark Burnhardt]].

## Profile

- **House**: Yellow Daffodil (Y)
- **Appearance**: Golden/blonde hair, plainly dressed
- **Family**: Sanzabar (listed under Gleadenhyme in Families doc)
- **Personality**: Quiet, observant, follows Lark's lead. "Reportedly hostile" per Johan's experience.

## Role

Derek serves as Lark's information-gatherer. At Maelfrey station, he checks the passenger list. After Alex's stream selection, Derek approaches to ask which stream Alex picked — information he likely relays to Lark/Lawrence.

He later switches streams (Act 2A planning: "Derek switches Stream 2k") — suggesting his initial placement may have been strategic rather than genuine interest.

## Significance

Derek represents the intelligence network around Lark. While Lark is the charismatic front, Derek handles logistics and reconnaissance. His family name "Sanzabar" echoes the Zanzibar nation conquered by Elven La.
