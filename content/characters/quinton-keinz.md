# Quinton Keinz

A Keinz (manaless human) who ranks at **SS tier** — the highest power level alongside Edmund Clive Gloomshard. Leader of the **Black Orchid Orphans** and operator of an orphanage temple.

## Profile

- **Species**: Human (Keinz — manaless)
- **Appearance**: Shabby black-haired man, smiling. Wears a SPECTR coat with numerous badges.
- **Power Tier**: SS (highest)
- **Affiliation**: SPECTR (formerly?), Black Orchid Orphanage Temple

## Role in Royce Trilogy

During the "New Dawn" arc (1912):
1. Levy and Royce's squad track assassin vampires
2. They capture **Boris Erlang** and a Black Orchid orphan
3. Boris escapes using Elven Augment
4. Levy is taken hostage to **Master Quinton's Orphanage Temple**
5. Royce is blindfolded and brought to meet Quinton

## Significance

- His SS-tier status despite being Keinz (manaless) proves Royce's philosophy: "Mana is not an indicator of power"
- Connected to both Levy and Royce (photograph of all three together in trophy room)
- The "Finding Quinton" arc title (1926) suggests he goes missing or must be located
- His surname "Keinz Joey" links him to the Blackheart faction in Harpei

## Connection to Harpei

In the Tales From Troha spinoff, the **Keinz Blackhearts** are led by "Quinton Keinz Joey" and his spy "Edward Keinz Joey" — suggesting Quinton has involvement with Harpei's underworld.
