# Shiro Yagurai

A first-year elf student at [[UYM Khinning]]. Youngest of the Yagurai brothers at the school.

## Profile

- **House**: Red Lotus (R)
- **Appearance**: Short, sharp face, short dark red hair, haughty pitch-black eyes
- **Family**: Yagurai — former royal guards of [[Elven La]] before the revolution. Brothers Saburo (senior, Fire King) and Jiro (senior) also attend UYM.
- **Personality**: Proud, silent, unfazed. Walks away from his brothers' pranks without reaction.

## Combat

Wins his sparring match against Billy Darnell in Chapter 15. His family's royal guard heritage gives him strong combat foundations. His main rivalry in the story is with **[[Johnny Sabat]]** — their brutal fight in Chapter 30.5 is told from Shiro's perspective. Johnny bullied Johan to provoke Shiro into a one-on-one fight.

## Connection to Ada

Ada mentions Shiro's family used to be royal guards before the Elvish rebellion. They are walking buddies, suggesting close friendship.
