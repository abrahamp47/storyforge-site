# Keon Berzgrandt

Professor of Year 1 Summoning/Conjury (Hunting Terrestrial) at [[UYM Khinning]]. Known as "The Fairy King."

## Profile

- **Species**: Lith (stony forehead, rocky fingers)
- **Power Tier**: S
- **Role**: Conjury professor, enrollment counter, Form Sleeve instructor
- **Family**: Berzgrandt (listed under Krillma in Families doc)
- **Demeanor**: Brilliantly dressed. Warm and encouraging with students.

## Teaching

- Runs Form Sleeve transformation classes
- Explains the "lift the mana clasp" technique for transformations
- Instructs students to switch forms using their specific creature sleeves
- Called "The Fairy King" — possibly his own conjury form or honorary title

## At Enrollment

First faculty member Alex meets at UYM. Sits at the registration counter, processes forms. Mentions Royce had told him about Alex. Offers to fill Alex's form for him (Alex declines).
