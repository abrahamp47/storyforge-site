# Louis von Levenhawk

Prince of [[Khinning]], younger brother of [[Levy von Levenhawk]]. Lives secretly in Royce's attic at the Renmark Estate while attending [[UYM Khinning]].

## Profile

- **Stream**: Fire Sorcery
- **House**: Red Lotus (R)
- **Age**: ~14 at Ivory Tower start (needs to be 18 to claim throne)
- **Personality**: Proud, maintains a facade of distance in class. Troubled by family trauma. Explores Khinning's provinces (notably Dinsprey) searching for a "lost waterfall."

## Backstory

- Parents killed by the **Black Hand** — a faction within the Khinning Military
- Brother Levy went searching for the Black Hand; "his body returned lifeless" (first death, 1913)
- Louis believes **Regent Lance** founded the Black Hand — "People say the Keinzlandt massacre was the cause, but I know he is behind it all"
- Lives with [[Royce Garett]] as guardian; Royce "doesn't believe my theory" about Lance
- Was hidden at Royce's estate for safety before joining UYM

## Role in the Story

Louis's paranoia about Lance and his claim to the throne create a subplot of political intrigue within the school arc. His friendship with Alex develops slowly — he envies Alex's stable family life.

## At UYM (Novel)

- Skips Stream Selection (name called, no one stands). Appears late on the first day of class in all black with a ponytail and sword.
- Challenges **Saburo Yagurai** (King of House Carnations) immediately. Uses fire sorcery ("Ignimoto" — flames from elbows for speed boost) but Saburo melts his sword blade. Loses but earns respect.
- Lives in Royce's attic; discovered by Alex, Aron, Emilie, and Nora during a midnight exploration (Ch 17). Fights them in the dark using sorcery and swordsmanship.
- Trains Alex in sword alignment: teaches the principle that "the edge aligned with the trajectory always cuts first" and magnetite's effect on sword physics in Harpei.
- Confrontation with Lark at the Golden Ring in Harpei — Lark insults his royal standing ("who can you call?"), Louis draws sword, Alex intervenes.
- Explores Dinsprey province searching for a "lost waterfall."

## Timeline

- 1926: Death of Lance → Regency of Louis (he becomes Regent briefly)
- 1927: Louis "steps out" → King Levy's second coronation
- Levy's final letter reveals Louis's coronation was planned: "With my death and subsequent crowning of Louis, the Westbrook pact will have been broken"
