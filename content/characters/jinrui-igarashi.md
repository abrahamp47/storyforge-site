# Jinrui Igarashi

Professor at [[UYM Khinning]]. Phase 3 Stream Selection counsellor and Language teacher.

## Profile

- **Species**: Elf
- **Power Tier**: S (alongside Levy and Keon)
- **Appearance**: Young elf, thick horn-rimmed glasses, golden pen, paper board. Shy but confident voice.
- **Role**: Phase 3 final choice counsellor, Elvish language teacher (listed as "H (Eng G)" — Human language teacher as well)
- **Family**: Igarashi (from Elven La)

## At Selection

Jinrui is the final counsellor who formally confirms a student's stream choice. His process:
1. Asks preferred stream
2. Records Phase 1 and Phase 2 suggestions
3. Confirms final choice
4. Shakes hand, announces enrollment

Notably comments "Great, Royce went overboard… again" when Alex reports 3 suggested streams — suggesting Royce tends to over-qualify students.

## S-Tier Power

Despite his scholarly appearance, Jinrui is rated S-tier — same as Levy and Keon. His combat capabilities are unexplored in current material but place him among the strongest characters.
