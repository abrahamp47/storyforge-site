# Randall Kilgrahm

A tragic figure from [[Khinning]]'s history — a soldier-turned-vampire whose vengeance destabilizes the kingdom.

## Backstory

Randall was part of the **Kilgrahm receiver family** within Khinning's monarchical system. Ordered by **King Lavoisier von Levenhawk** to massacre a group of vampires terrorizing the populace, Randall succeeded — but one vampire escaped and infected his family as revenge.

When Lavoisier discovered vampires in the Kilgrahm household, he ordered their extermination. Randall tried to protect his family but all were killed except his sister. They barricaded themselves in an inaccessible room; soldiers sealed the house. Starving, his sister bit him. Randall's next memory is consuming his sister's remains while half his own body had been eaten.

## Transformation

The trauma drove Randall to hatred of the King. He hibernated to regenerate his body, then attacked and killed King Lavoisier and his wife. This act triggers [[Levy von Levenhawk]]'s vendetta against him.

## Philosophy

> "Spite... Pity... Envy... Fear... Hatred. These are human emotions born out of inequality. I want a world without hate, a world without suffering. A world of equivalence, where the weakest Keinz and the strongest Manathist are one and the same. Perpetual life, where all are equals. I can achieve that with vampirism."

Randall's ideology positions vampirism as a path to equality — eternal life removes the power differential between the mana-gifted and the manaless. He is both monster and idealist.
