# Lawrence

A minister of [[Khinning]] who serves as political mentor to [[Lark Burnhardt]] and takes an early interest in Prince [[Alexander Gloomshard]].

## Profile

- **Appearance**: Middle-aged, aristocratic bearing. Red velvet robe, embroidered black cape with ochre borders, black tricorn hat. Broad-shouldered, strong yet civilized.
- **Role**: Minister from Khinning; attends Alex's knighting ceremony in Gleadenhyme. Keen to meet the prince.
- **Manner**: "Bright homely smile." Uses Khinning salutations.

## Philosophy & Methods

Revealed through chess dialogues with Lark:
- Teaches logic, emotional restraint, finding flaws in people.
- Assigned Lark to make Alex an ally; disappointed that Lark tried domination instead.
- Views Lark's ego as his critical weakness.
- Strategic thinker who plays long political games.

## Role in the Story

Lawrence represents the Khinning political establishment's interest in Gleadenhyme's crown prince. His presence at the knighting ceremony and eagerness to meet Alex suggest he is positioning political pieces well before the events at UYM.
