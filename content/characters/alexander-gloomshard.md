# Alexander Marcus Gloomshard

Crown Prince of [[Gleadenhyme]], eldest child of King [[House Gloomshard|Marcus Finneas Gloomshard]]. Protagonist of *The Ivory Tower*.

## Profile

- **Age**: 16 at story start (born 1908, story begins 1923)
- **Stream**: Conjury (Gangine form)
- **School**: [[UYM Khinning]], admitted rank 53/5000
- **Personality**: Introverted, thoughtful, struggles with attention and public ceremonies. Tends to brood on rooftops. Reserved but deeply emotional beneath the surface.
- **Family**: House Gloomshard, rulers of Gleadenhyme.

## Arc

Alex leaves the sheltered life of Gloomshard Manor to study at UYM Khinning. His journey is one of self-discovery — choosing his Stream, navigating political rivalries between the Five Nations' students, and confronting threats that connect to the broader political upheaval across Troha.

## Key Relationships

- **[[Emilie Gloomshard]]** — Cousin, cheerful foil to his introversion. Studies Alchemy. Travels with him to Khinning.
- **[[Royce Garett]]** — History professor and assigned mentor/security. White-haired with red pupils (Scarlet Scirocco).
- **[[House Gloomshard|Adrian Marcus Gloomshard]]** — Younger brother, enthusiastic about tarot and divination.
- **[[Lark Burnhardt]]** — Rival/antagonist at UYM, Wendigo conjurer, mentored by [[Lawrence]].

## Conjury Form: Gangine

Alex's transformation is the **Gangine** (aka Astomi):
- Humanoid creature without a mouth, 1.5–2m tall
- Red, gold, and blue skin with simple repeating paisley patterns
- Floats a few centimeters off the ground
- Heightened reflexes — dodges instinctively
- Does not communicate (no mouth); white eyes with no visible focus
- Fists curled, arms close to sides — initially unresponsive until Alex integrates his consciousness

First transformation occurs in Arc #6 (Mind Games) during Professor Keon's Form Sleeve class. Alex enters a dark internal world (pitch black, shallow water floor) where he must connect to the form.

## Power & Growth

- **Power Tier**: D (one of the weakest in the main cast)
- Struggles with mana projection (can't bridge air gap in orange exercise initially)
- Accidentally channels mana along surfaces rather than through air
- His growth from D-tier to eventual King (1929) is the core character journey

## Becomes King

Alexander is crowned King of [[Gleadenhyme]] in **1929** at age 22, making him the 11th Gloomshard ruler. This coincides with the "Empty Crown" arc and Levy's assassination.
