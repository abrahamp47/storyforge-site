# Hades Elfman

A student at [[UYM Khinning]] from [[Volta]]'s royal family.

## Profile

- **House**: Green Zinnia (G)
- **Power Tier**: B (above Lark and Louis)
- **Family**: Elfman — associated with Volta. "Aegis Elfman" attended the 1911 Round Table Summit (likely father/uncle).
- **Identifying Feature**: Wears a white shoulder cape with a golden torch (symbol of Voltian royalty)
- **Personality**: "Reportedly hostile" (per Johan). Doesn't engage easily.

## Backstory (Chapter 26.5)

Hades's tragic military history is revealed during the Dinsprey hunt:
- Son of **Theus Elfman**, a legendary hero ("master of storms")
- Elder brother: **First Sergeant Heres Elfman** — the one who truly knew their father
- Aunt fears the brothers taking her power; forgot they were children, only saw "sons of the true King"
- Enrolled in military academy; **Sergeant Ralphe** exploited him as a prize fighter
- People bet on his Sunday fights; eventually killed **Florya Kosach** (Cadet 24601) — a boy who held back 16 times before stumbling
- Dishonourably discharged at court-martial
- Heres's lesson: "Only the truly strong can afford to let go"
- Gift of **electromancy** from Voltian Military — sworn to reject it

## At UYM

- Combat class (Ch 15): Refuses to fight Keito ("I do not see it fit to engage someone weaker"). Punches Thunder Bruce fast enough to push the giant back inches.
- Dinsprey hunt (Ch 26.5): Defeats 4 ambushers (Fidel Warbrandt/Treant, Erick Studer/Gloomwing, Alan Breguet/Weaver, Milo Erlang/Ursa) who tried to steal his catches. Brutally efficient. Spares Akasuki as lookout.
- Electromancy sparks emerge during rage — he suppresses them, trying to honor Heres's teaching.

## Notable

- Pointy hair, intense eyes, white shoulder cape with golden torch (Voltian royal insignia)
- Father Aegis Elfman attended 1911 Round Table Summit (Theus likely = Aegis)
- His B-tier ranking + backstory makes him the most physically dangerous student
