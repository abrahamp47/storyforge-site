# Baron Yozaki Van Krill

Revolutionary dictator of [[Elven La]]. A half-blood elf who overthrew the Pureblood ruling system through populist revolution.

## Origins

Born to **Yuan Li Argesfoldt** (1st generation rock elf from [[Krillma]]) and **Kahn Li Zhao** (Pureblood elf merchant). Product of assault — Kahn rejected paternity. Yuan became the family maid. Yozaki was raised alongside Kahn's legitimate sons but constantly beaten by his father. Named himself "Van Krill" (from Kahn's slur "Olven canis van Krill" — "half-breed dog from Krillma") after his mother's death.

## Rise to Power

1. Excelled in history and languages at school; studied Ark's Law and concluded it was meant to include new families.
2. Attended the Elven Mana Domina Academy; befriended **Arkin Lee Yang** (Pureblood sympathizer).
3. Mastered **Elven Hemataugment** (Strength specialization).
4. Distinguished himself in the Southern Expansion war; earned Hero title.
5. **Korvan Lee Yang** blocked his advancement out of jealousy, ending the war prematurely.
6. Imprisoned for marching on the capitol with his troops.
7. After release, built a following among half-bloods; his "New Ark's Law" became popular.
8. Launched coup during the Jinshen-to-Lee-Yang power transition; murdered and drow'd opponents.
9. Took title of **Baron** (~1913).

## Rule

- Rejected by the Round Table — Elven La has no seat during his reign.
- Hunts the lost Lee Yang heir (identified by scar between shoulder blades).
- Drows political enemies (strips them of mana).
- Employs **[[Tansen Li Wei]]** for propaganda.
- Replaced as Baron by **Baroness Nora Lee Yang** in 1927.

## Zanzibar Massacre (1904)

Led the army that killed 100,000+ people at the Drogba Festival in Amatsu Corridor, Zanzibar. A child born in the fire survived unscathed — prophesied to bring Elven La's downfall (the "Firebrand").

## Philosophy

Sees himself as a liberator of half-bloods and immigrants against Pureblood supremacy. His methods are brutal but his cause has legitimate grievances. Complex villain — oppressed becomes oppressor.
