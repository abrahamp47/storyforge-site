# Ada Braunholdt

A student at [[UYM Khinning]] and Alex's primary love interest. Distinguished by her auburn hair and vivid green (viridian) eyes.

## Profile

- **House**: Blue Hyacinth (B)
- **Appearance**: Auburn locks, viridian (emerald green) eyes, slightly sheepish demeanor
- **Introduced by**: [[Johan Jordis]] during the enrollment line at UYM

## First Encounter

Alex first sees Ada through two carriage windows on Market Street in Central Khinning (Chapter 8). Her "alluring viridian orbs" and melancholic expression captivate him. Aron deduces she must be a UYM student based on her age, wealth (coach), and location. Alex confesses interest to Aron to prevent him from asking Emilie for "wingman" help.

## At UYM

Ada is already enrolled when Alex arrives for Stream Selection. Johan introduces them formally ("Hands... now."). She is sheepish but friendly. She walks home with Alex multiple times (Act 2B chapters 6–8).

## Significance

The romance subplot begins in Act 2A (chapters 20–25 per the rough structure). Ada appears to be central to the emotional arc of the middle section of Book 1.
