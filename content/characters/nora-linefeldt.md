# Nora Linefeldt

An elf student at [[UYM Khinning]], twin sister of [[Aron Linefeldt]]. One of Royce's core group.

## Profile

- **Species**: Elf (full mana — unlike her drow twin)
- **House**: Green Zinnia (G)
- **Personality**: Blunt, harsh, critical. Already knows mana activation before training. Pushes others hard.
- **Skills**: Advanced mana control from the start. Fastest at exercises.

## Relationship with Aron

Despite her harsh exterior, Nora's criticisms of Aron stem from concern — she knows he's weaker without mana and fears he'll kill himself training. Their dynamic as mana-having twin vs. drow twin is a core tension.

## Notable

- The name "Nora Lee Yang" appears in the timeline as **Baroness** of Elven La (1927–present). If this is the same Nora, she eventually returns to claim her Elven La heritage.
