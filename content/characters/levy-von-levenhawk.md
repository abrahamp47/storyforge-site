# Levy von Levenhawk

King of [[Khinning]], son of King Levant of House Levenhawk. A tragic figure whose reign is bookended by violence.

## Profile

- **Born**: ~1894 (age 1 in Levy column of Timeline)
- **Crowned (first)**: 1911, after King Levant is wounded in the "Coffin Rebirth" event
- **Death (first reign)**: 1913 — dies during "Another World's Lament"; Regency of Lance begins
- **Crowned (second)**: 1927 — returns as King after Louis steps down
- **Assassinated**: 1929 — "Empty Crown" event

## Arc

Levy inherits the throne young after his father Levant is grievously wounded. His first reign is cut short by death in 1913, leading to a regency under his brother Lance. After Lance's death (1926) and Louis's brief regency, Levy is somehow restored to the throne in 1927, only to be assassinated two years later.

## Connection to Randall Kilgrahm

Levy's father, King Lavoisier (Levant), ordered the massacre of [[Randall Kilgrahm]]'s vampiric family. Randall's vengeance targets the royal couple. Levy must hunt the vampire who destroyed his parents, unaware of the tragic backstory.

## Political Significance

The instability of Khinning's throne (Levant → Levy → Lance → Louis → Levy → assassination) creates the political backdrop for the events at [[UYM Khinning]] during the Ivory Tower arc (1923).
