# Royce J. Garett

Professor of History at [[UYM Khinning]] and assigned mentor/security for Prince [[Alexander Gloomshard]] and [[Emilie Gloomshard]].

## Profile

- **Full Name**: Royce "Jariel" Garett Renmark
- **Appearance**: Thin frame, white/snow hair, shiny red/crimson pupils. Pale skin that steams slightly in sunlight. No older than twenty at story start.
- **Alias**: Scarlet Scirocco
- **Role**: History professor, carriage driver (uses magic to summon/dismiss carriages), security escort.
- **Power Tier**: A
- **Residence**: [[Renmark Estate]]
- **Philosophy**: "Never give up on people, on effort — never on yourself. Make love and war, finding the balance is the key."
- **Notable**: Portrait of "Robert Renmark and Royce Renmark" in trophy room suggests family lineage at the estate.

## Timeline Connection

Per the master timeline:
- 1909: Royce meets Levy (age ~16/2)
- 1910: Joins Lance's unit (age ~17/3)
- 1915: Royce learns at UYM (age ~22/8)
- 1919: Royce joins as teacher (age ~26/12)
- 1923: Ivory Tower events begin; Royce escorts Alex to Khinning

## Teaching Style

- Teaches mana activation through meditation (listen to heartbeat → feel the "spring" → channel to hands)
- "Coat trick" — telekinetic pull of objects using projected mana through air
- Assigns extra physical training to [[Aron Linefeldt]] to compensate for his lack of mana
- Orange-peeling exercise: condense mana to fingertips, stretch it across air gap to the orange
- Summoning/dismissing carriages with a finger snap
- Rule 8 of UYM Conduct: "No manathism without teacher permission (except Prof. Royce J. Garett)" — implying his students have special freedom

## Significance

Despite his young and slight appearance, Royce is trusted by King Marcus to protect the Crown Prince. His red eyes and white hair mark him as physically unusual — steams in sunlight, suggesting vampiric or undead nature ("ghost boy" per Levy's letter). His A-tier power rating places him above all his students. His early connection to Levy and Lance, and his role protecting Louis, make him central to Khinning's royal politics.

## Levy's Final Letter

Addressed to "my dearest friend Royce" — Levy asks Royce to ensure Louis never learns that Levy's death was self-orchestrated. Levy calls him "ghost boy" affectionately. Royce is Levy's most trusted person.
