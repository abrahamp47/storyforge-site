# Tansen Li Wei

E-pop singer and propaganda operative for Baron [[Yozaki Van Krill]]'s regime in [[Elven La]].

## Profile

- **Role**: Singer, mononymously known as "Tansen"; part of the media/public image (propaganda) division.
- **Appearance**: Has a significant scar on his back between his shoulder blades.
- **Family**: Li Wei clan — orthodox elven family from [[Krillma]] that migrated to Elven La 34 years after Ark's Law. Not part of the ruling Pureblood families.

## The Identity Twist

- His father died in the squabbles between higher elves and the Krill Army (fought for Krill side).
- Grew up as a Krill (Van Krill) fanatic, seeing purple-skinned high elves as evil.
- His mother, Mrs Li Wei, is revealed to be a **high elf traitor** who rescued the true heir to the throne — the child of **Korvan Lee Yang** — and let the child escape to Khinning.
- Tansen denounces her as a "purple skinned traitor"; Yozaki drows her.
- Yozaki's identification mark for the lost prince: **scar between the shoulder blades**.
- The story ends when Tansen realizes the identification mark matches his own.

## Significance

Tansen may unknowingly be the lost Lee Yang prince he has been helping Yozaki hunt. His identity as a Krill propaganda figure who is actually the heir to the overthrown dynasty creates a devastating irony.
