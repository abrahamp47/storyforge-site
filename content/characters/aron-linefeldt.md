# Aron Linefeldt

A drow elf student at [[UYM Khinning]], twin brother of Nora. One of Royce's core group of students. Secretly the son of **Korvan Lee Yang** (Elven La general).

## Profile

- **Species**: Elf (Drow — manaless)
- **Stream**: Tinkery (Manatechnology) — the only stream viable for a manaless student
- **House**: Blue Hyacinth (B)
- **Personality**: Quiet, determined, physically pushes himself to compensate for lack of mana. Dry humor.
- **Power Tier**: E (lowest among main cast)

## The Drow's Burden

As a drow, Aron possesses no mana. He cannot participate in mana activation exercises, can't heal his own wounds with mana, and has lower stamina without mana-boosting. Royce compensates by assigning extra physical training (laps). Aron pushes to dangerous limits — nearly dying at 27 laps when his safe maximum is 25.

## Secret Heritage

In Arc #2, Aron recognizes his father **Korvan Lee Yang** in the Round Table painting at the Renmark Estate. Korvan appears "not purple but slightly tanned" — meaning he was not visibly a high elf. This connects Aron to [[Elven La]]'s deposed royalty and the broader revolution plotline.

- **Card games**: "The Drow King" — wins at Block by strategy
- **Voice of empathy**: Defends Keito's perspective to Lark ("Everyone has a story"), explains the orphan's trauma
