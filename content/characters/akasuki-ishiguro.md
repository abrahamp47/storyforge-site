# Akasuki Ishiguro

A Lith student at [[UYM Khinning]] from the exiled Ishiguro clan of the [[Society of Elven Hope]].

## Profile

- **Species**: Lith (family shifted from Elf to Lith over generations)
- **House**: Green Zinnia (G)
- **Conjury Form**: Greentooth (Siren)
- **Personality**: Fiery, proud of her heritage, quick to defend others. Broad smile, fierce temper.
- **Nickname**: "Suki" — "short and sweet"

## Heritage: The Great Ishiguro

The Ishiguro clan was one of the original 30 families of Elven Hope — next in line after the Li Wei family. When her ancestor proposed allowing immigrants (Laowais) into the courts, the clan was excommunicated, branded as traitors and blamed for corruption. The Jinshens "stepped over us to get to the throne."

## Role in the Story

- Fights Keito Jin Shen after he insults Drows (defending Nora's brother)
- Gets pushed onto the Tatzelwurm cage by Keito (poisoned but survives — Sodsroth is less harmful to Liths)
- Alex explains summoning to her as his pretext to learn about the Keito conflict
- During the Dinsprey hunt (Chapter 26.5), she acts as lookout from a watchpost; blows emergency whistle when Hades goes berserk; Hades shushes her and leaves

## Significance

Suki represents the Ishiguro side of the Elven La political triangle: Jinshens (Keito) vs Ishiguro (Suki) vs Van Krill's revolution. Her Lith heritage is the physical proof of why the Elven Hope excluded them.
