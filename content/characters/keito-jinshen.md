# Keito Jin Shen

An elven student at [[UYM Khinning]], descendant of the deposed Jinshen ruling clan of [[Elven La]].

## Profile

- **Species**: Elf (former Pureblood)
- **Background**: Parents killed by revolutionaries during [[Yozaki Van Krill]]'s coup. Orphaned survivor of the Jinshen massacre.
- **Personality**: Arrogant, angry, prejudiced against Drows and Liths (whom he blames for the revolution). Deep trauma beneath the surface.

## Actions

- Mouths off about Drows, provoking [[Nora Linefeldt]]
- Fights [[Akasuki Ishiguro]] (Lith from excommunicated Ishiguro clan) — the name alone enrages him
- Pushes Suki onto the Tatzelwurm cage (unknowingly endangering her)
- Challenges [[Aron Linefeldt]] to a fight in Thunder Bruce's combat class — loses
- Shows a flash of genuine horror when told the Tatzelwurm poison could kill Suki

## Aron's Perspective

Aron sees Keito not as purely evil but as a traumatized child who "never found justice, never found solace." He hates those who benefitted from his parents' death. "To a little boy, good and bad hold no value, especially after 'The Good' left him forsaken."

## Connection to Elven La Succession

The "Ark Jin Shen" name appears on the Students' List — likely Keito enrolled under the clan's formal name. His clan created Ark's Law; they feel entitled to Elven La's throne.
