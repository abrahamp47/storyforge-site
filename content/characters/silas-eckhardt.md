# Silas Eckhardt

Phase 2 Stream Selection counsellor at [[UYM Khinning]]. A mind-reader and swordsman wielding a Seafarer's Giant.

## Profile

- **Physical (external)**: Ashen, corpse-like. Pale sickly man with greyed hair, no shirt, bandages around belly. Skeleton visible through skin. Holds a tall black pole (disguised sword sheath). Bright topaz eyes. Speaks in a feeble trembling voice.
- **Mental/Internal form**: Healthy, strong, well-defined body. Long silver hair. Bright red pants. Youthful, confident voice. Carries the unwrapped Seafarer's Giant sword.
- **Ability**: "Kraken Bound" — reads memories and thoughts. Can pull people into their own mindscape. His mind-reading works on active thoughts (firing neurons) and deeper memories.
- **Weapon**: The Seafarer's Giant — a legendary sword, two men tall, long (not broad), flaming. One of the fabled Seafarer's swords.

## Role in Selection

Silas examines students' memories and mental aptitude. For Alex:
- Rapidly scans Alex's memories (Lark, Volta boy, viridian eyes, Zefua, Moon White, train, Redford, knighting)
- Takes Alex to his "calm place" (atop Gloomshard Manor)
- Tests his combat instinct with sword challenges
- Alex's breakthrough: attacking without premeditation (instinct over thought)
- Suggests Conjury: "You always think of another way. It is a key value for a great Conjurer."
- Parting words: "Levi wishes you past his grave"

## Connection to Levy

Silas was part of Levy's squad during the Royce Trilogy era. His mind-reading ability was used to interrogate Boris Erlang (who broke free by absorbing the mana space).
