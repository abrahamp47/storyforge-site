# Emilie Connor Gloomshard

Cousin of [[Alexander Gloomshard]] and fellow student at [[UYM Khinning]]. The cheerful, confident foil to Alex's introversion.

## Profile

- **Stream**: Alchemy ("The secrets of the universe are hidden in alchemy")
- **Family**: Daughter of the Royal Alchemist of Gleadenhyme (Uncle Connor)
- **Personality**: Bold, sassy, energetic, confident. Calls Alex "Princy" or "Prince Boy." Physically demonstrative (jumps on Alex's back as a greeting).

## Arc

Emilie passes the UYM entrance exam and travels with Alex to Khinning. She believes she will inherit her father's position as Royal Alchemist (though the position is merit-based, not hereditary). Her enthusiasm and social confidence counterbalance Alex's reserved nature.

## Alchemical Talent

Emilie demonstrates exceptional alchemical thinking:
- Diagnoses Tatzelwurm (Sodsroth) poisoning in Zefua by observing artery dilation vs nerve/mana duct patterns
- Wins potion contest against Nora by inventing a **new wound-healing formula**: Tatzelwurm venom + mana-enhanced Witch Hazel + enhanced skin cells. Creates a paralytic wound sealant.
- Sources illegal Tatzelwurm venom from Valenu Snicket (black market) in Ol' Bottle Street
- Royce's lesson: "An alchemist thinks" — Emilie creates, Nora follows recipes

## Focused Mana Expression

Her mana focus point is **fingertips** (appropriate for alchemy — precise channeling for reagent work).

## Notes

- Performs the "guess who" trick on Alex for 4 years straight.
- Her parents' farewell is notably brief compared to Alex's emotional departure.
- Has a chance encounter with an elven guitarist who resembles [[Tansen Li Wei]].
- Initially terrified of Sodvics (believes they are devil worshippers), but overcomes this in Zefua.
- Competitive with [[Nora Linefeldt]] — their rivalry becomes a growth catalyst for both.
