# Kenichi Yagurai

Grand Guard of the Sodvic Republic of Zefua. An elf and friend of [[Royce Garett]].

## Profile

- **Species**: Elf
- **Appearance**: Very muscular, early 30s, distinctly messy blonde hair, matching beard. Broad shoulders. Wears a vibrant mauve, murray, and malachite coat.
- **Role**: Grand Guard of Zefua; meets and greets sojourners, leads them to the Chief
- **Personality**: Cordial, soft-voiced, ceremonial. Happy with quiet life in the woods.

## Connection to Royce

Knows Royce as "Jariel of Tonopo" — one of Royce's aliases used while traveling incognito. Their relationship is warm and familiar; Royce visits regularly.

## Zefua

The Sodvic Republic of Zefua is the 12th non-principal country, allied with [[Gleadenhyme]] and King Marcus Finneas Gloomshard. It's a settlement in the woods between Wyndale and Khinning — colorful, vibrant culture emphasizing peaceful coexistence despite the ominous exterior walls and Sodvic religious imagery.

## Family

Part of the **Yagurai** family (from [[Elven La]]). Shiro Yagurai and Kinshi Yagurai (UYM Close Combat teacher) are likely relatives.
