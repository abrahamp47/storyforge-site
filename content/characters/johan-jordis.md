# Johan Jordis

A student at [[UYM Khinning]] from Krieksburg, [[Krillma]]. Enthusiastic, earnest, and slightly eccentric.

## Profile

- **House**: Blue Hyacinth (B)
- **Nickname**: Jojo ("A bit bizarre, but catchy")
- **Origin**: Krieksburg, Krillma
- **Personality**: Earnest, loves introducing people to each other. Has a "slightly eccentric smile" and a "pleasant tone that gives creeps." Hates waiting.

## Role

Johan is the social connector — he introduces Alex to [[Ada Braunholdt]] during the enrollment line. He enjoys "making people meet" and considers it cool. He participates in a tournament later in the story (Act 2B: "Johan's Tournament admission").

## Notable

- First student Alex befriends at UYM besides his Renmark Estate group
- Contrasted with Lark: both are socially forward, but Johan is genuinely warm while Lark has ulterior motives
